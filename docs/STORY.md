# STORY · VALLEYWARD (binding for every text, line and hint in the game)

## The truth (never stated in the game until Night X)
The player is a **fire demon** wearing the shape of a lamplighter. The "beacon" on the south-east tower is the
**Hellfire Lamp**: every night it burns, it opens the way a little wider. The goblins of the valley are a peaceful folk
who know what the lamp is; every night they climb the hill to **put it out**. After ten nights the lamp will be bright
enough, and the **demon host** will march out of it into the valley. The player is not defending a keep. They are
guarding a door.

## The rule for hints
Everything must have an innocent reading **and** a dark one. A player who suspects nothing should see a grim fantasy
siege; a player who notices should feel the floor tilt. Never say "demon", "hell" or "summon" in any text before
Night X (the item is the **Hellfire Lamp** only in the internal name; on screen it is "the lamp" or "the beacon").

## Hints, in the order a player meets them
1. **You do not burn.** Fire washes over your boots and nothing happens; your gloves smoulder at the fingertips when the
   Lance fires (a faint ember glow on the glove seams).
2. **Their banners.** The Goblin Chieftain's banner shows a lamp **crossed out** (on his sprite, and dropped banners on
   the slope).
3. **The water.** Night II brings Dousers with buckets: they are not attacking the keep, they are trying to put out
   fire (mechanic + sprite).
4. **Brother Cinder.** He never says what he is. Lines (subtitles in his panel, voiced where a voice line exists):
   - "You're home, Lamplighter. The lamp missed you."
   - "Coin for the hide? It wears thin on you. Always has."
   - "They climb because they're afraid. Afraid is good. Afraid is loud."
   - "Don't mind the gloves. They smoke when you're cross."
   - "Eight more nights. Then we can all go out." (after Night II)
   - "Odd. I never see you eat."
   - On a fall: "You'll be back. You always are. That's rather the point of you."
5. **The lamp.** When you kindle it, for half a second the flames show **horned silhouettes marching** (in the
   kindle glance; subtle, small).
6. **Dawn cards.** Night I: "DAWN · THE WATCH HOLDS" / sub-line "The valley is quieter tonight." Nights II–IX: "THE LAMP
   DRINKS THE NIGHT" / "<Eight> more nights, and it will be bright enough.", counting down to Night X.
7. **The moon** reddens each night (Night I teal-white, Night II blood-red, Night X a black disc).
8. **Your shadow** at dusk in the camp has horns (the low western sun throws the player's shadow on the wall; a
   tiny sprite shadow with two horns).
9. **The goblins' cries** (optional floating pixel speech bubbles over a few goblins, rare):
   "PUT IT OUT!", "FOR THE VALLEY!", "THE LAMP! THE LAMP!" — never "DEMON".

## Night X (docs/DESIGN.md §6.3, §12, §12.9)
The goblins' last hope is **the Rainmother**, an ancient river-giantess they wake to drown the lamp. When she falls, the
ending plays (DESIGN §12) and the rule against saying "demon" ends at the final card. Brother Cinder's last line:
"Welcome home, Master." Then the hunt through the valley and the credits ("Directed by AL"); the game ends (no
post-ending world).
